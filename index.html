    About PT Sarana Indah Logistic

    Freight Forwarding Company in Surabaya

    PT Sarana Indah Logistic is a freight forwarding and logistics company based in Surabaya, East Java. We help businesses move their goods through export and import shipments, domestic cargo, and supporting logistics services.

    • Sea freight and air freight for domestic and international shipments.
    • Trucking and cargo handling between ports, warehouses, and destinations.
    • Customs clearance support for smooth and compliant cargo processing.

    Operating from Surabaya, one of Indonesia's main trade gateways, we coordinate shippers, carriers, ports, and destinations so your cargo arrives safely and on schedule. Contact us for freight forwarding and export import services tailored to your cargo.
