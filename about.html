PT Sarana Indah Logistic is a freight forwarding company in Surabaya that helps businesses ship export, import, and domestic cargo with clarity, speed, and care.

A Surabaya freight forwarder built around your cargo

We operate from Jl. Embong Malang in the heart of Surabaya, one of Indonesia's busiest trade gateways. Every shipment is different, so we start with your cargo, your schedule, and your destination, then plan the route that fits.

One team for the whole journey

Moving goods usually means juggling shippers, shipping lines, ports, truckers, and customs. We coordinate all of them, so you deal with one responsive team instead of many.

Connected to leading shipping lines

We work with global liners including Evergreen, OOCL, COSCO, ONE, Gold Star Line, KMTC, and CMA CGM, giving your cargo access to reliable sea routes.

From booking to delivery, our logistics services cover the steps your cargo needs to arrive safely and on schedule.

Sea freight and air freight

FCL and LCL container shipments by sea for domestic and international destinations, plus air freight for time-sensitive cargo that cannot wait.

Trucking and cargo handling

Land transportation between ports, warehouses, and final destinations, supported by loading, unloading, and cargo handling services.

Customs clearance support

Help with customs processing (jasa kepabeanan) so your export and import cargo moves through smoothly and in line with regulations.

See all our logistics services

Reliable freight forwarding is about more than moving boxes. It is about keeping you informed and your cargo on track.

Experience with many kinds of cargo

Timber and wood products, general cargo, oversize and heavy cargo, containers, frozen fish and shrimp, ceramics, palm oil, spices, medical equipment, paper, charcoal, and briquettes. We know that each cargo type needs its own handling.

Fast, direct communication

Ask a question and get an answer from a real person. Reach us directly on WhatsApp or email for quotes, shipment updates, and documentation questions.

Solutions tailored to your business

Whether you ship one container or run regular export and import programs, we shape our service around your needs, budget, and timeline.

Planning a shipment from or to Surabaya? Tell us about your cargo and we will help you find the right route.
